# Example Rubric: Senior Visual Designer

A filled-in example to show what a good rubric looks like. Replace everything with details from your own intake meeting.

```
ROLE RUBRIC
Role: Senior Visual Designer
Level: Senior
Team / context: Small brand and marketing design team. This person will own visual design for product launches, the website, and campaign assets, working closely with marketing and product.

Must-haves (any score of 1–2 here keeps a candidate out of Tier A):
1. Strong visual hierarchy, layout, and typography
2. Shipped brand or marketing work for a real product or company
3. Shows process (sketches, iterations, or reasoning), not just final images

Nice-to-haves:
1. Motion or animation work
2. Experience building or using a design system
3. Web design that went live

Red flags to note (not automatic disqualifiers):
- Only student or concept work for a senior role
- No explanation of their role on team projects

What "great" looks like, in the hiring manager's words:
"Someone who can take a rough launch brief and come back with a clear, polished direction, and explain why they made the choices they did."
```
