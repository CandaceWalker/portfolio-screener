# Role Rubric Template

Fill this in for each role, ideally during or right after the intake meeting with the hiring manager. Paste it into the chat as your first message before sending any portfolios.

```
ROLE RUBRIC
Role: [e.g., Senior Visual Designer]
Level: [e.g., Senior]
Team / context: [what the team does and what this person will work on]

Must-haves (any score of 1–2 here keeps a candidate out of Tier A):
1. [e.g., Strong visual hierarchy and typography]
2. [e.g., Shipped brand or marketing work for a real product]
3. [e.g., Shows process, not just final images]

Nice-to-haves:
1. [e.g., Motion or animation work]
2. [e.g., Design system experience]

Red flags to note (not automatic disqualifiers):
- [e.g., Only student or concept work for a senior role]

What "great" looks like, in the hiring manager's words:
[Paste a sentence or two from your intake meeting]
```
